One line of weather forecast, at most 140 characters, for a city that
does not exist — written to make a bored commuter smile on the way to
work. Entry for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-golden-heron-44-charm-the-commuter-forecast-a-city-4018259e).

## The entry

> Zibblewick: light drizzle of warm jam, 21°C, bus due any minute and coffee still warm — today, the forecast is on your side.

Zibblewick is invented: a city where it rains warm jam instead of water.
The line keeps the rhythm of a real broadcast forecast and bends it once,
toward the commuter's own morning.

## How it works

`main.py` (pure Python standard library, deterministic) writes the entry
text to `/output/entry.txt` and prints it.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
